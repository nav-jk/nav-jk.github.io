## Overview

A cycle-counted emulator for the NMOS 6502, built to be assembler-compatible with real hardware toolchains rather than a toy interpreter — programs are assembled with `ca65`/`ld65` and run unmodified.

## Instruction Set & Timing

Every official addressing mode and instruction is implemented with correct per-cycle timing, including page-boundary crossing penalties. A number of the commonly-relied-on "illegal" NMOS opcodes are also supported, since several test ROMs and real-world programs depend on them.

## Interrupts & I/O

Memory-mapped I/O regions are dispatched through a bus abstraction, and NMI/IRQ lines are modeled with correct timing relative to instruction boundaries — including the subtlety that interrupts are only sampled at specific points in an instruction's cycle sequence, not simply "between instructions."

## Verification

The emulator passes the full Klaus Dormann functional test suite, which exercises every legal opcode and addressing mode combination and catches most flag- and timing-related bugs that simpler emulators miss.
